## Pass and feedback

All nine lesson numerical tasks must be correct after any number of revisions, with a 30-character minimum reflection and self-review acknowledgement per lesson. This is mastery-oriented practice, not a punitive score. Incorrect numerical practice gives a method hint first, then the worked explanation on subsequent checks. Question-level attempts are retained. Confidence is optional and ungraded.

The independent check has eight items in two fixed cases, seven numeric and one multiple-choice. Pass requires 7/8, including cumulative return a3, exact real return a7, and conditional-evidence claim a8. A specific correction/check response is required after a submitted pass. Answers changed after grading require a new submission. Submitted attempts remain in the export. Prose minimum lengths validate completeness, not reasoning quality.

Model answers and worked explanations are declared in model.mjs. Case A: 8%; £5,618; 0%; 10%; £1,088.44; £981.41; −2.80%; conditional-input claim (second option). Case B: 5%; £899.89; −1%; 10%; £1,333.49; £981.14; −2.83%; conditional-input claim (second option).

Lab default nominal £1,628.89, simple interest £1,500.00, real balance £1,212.05, separate £2,000 payment PV £1,227.83. Changing only inflation to 6% leaves nominal and PV unchanged and reduces purchasing power. Full precision retained until presentation. All inputs synthetic, deterministic; no random seed or historical split applies. Time 0 deposit, annual effective rates and reinvestment, no later deposits, fees or taxes. No return-based marks.

## Calculation-card self-review / future human rubric

Score four dimensions 0/1/2: reproducible inputs and dates; correct units and formula; meaningful independent check; assumptions and feedback response. 0 = absent or materially wrong; 1 = partly explicit; 2 = specific and traceable. Browser prose is self-reviewed; the app does not apply this rubric as an expert judgement. For consequential assessment, add human review and an appeal route.

Strong exemplar (8/8 under this rubric): £1,000 at time 0, 5% annual effective growth and 3% inflation over 10 years, no external flows or costs. Report £1,628.89 nominal and £1,212.05 in starting-year pounds. State the compound formula, independent arithmetic check and inflation-only comparison. Replace a future-return guarantee with a conditional model claim.

Weak exemplar (0–1/8): “5 + 5 means 10% after two years; inflation is irrelevant because the balance rose, so this guarantees a better investment.” Correct the changing base, real-value interpretation and guarantee before asserting competence. Positive hypothetical growth earns no marks by itself.

Two examples are author calibration material, not evidence of two-human scoring reliability. Suggested pacing and thresholds still need a learner pilot. No credential, accreditation, current return forecast or commercial release readiness is claimed.
