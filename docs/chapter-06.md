The one-month return uses beginning-of-month long-only fractions summing to1 and same-date returns. Allocation concentration isH=wA²+wB² and is explicitly distinct from complete risk. Monthly constant-mix resets to target before each following monthly return; buy-and-hold compounds initial asset sleeves and permits weight drift. Costs, taxes, flows and borrowing are omitted. Pearson correlation and beta use matching monthly simple-return pairs; beta is sample covariance/sample benchmark variance, with n−1 cancelling. A constant benchmark makes beta undefined; constant portfolio with varying benchmark has beta0 and undefined correlation. Exact all-identical guards prevent floating-point false variance.
